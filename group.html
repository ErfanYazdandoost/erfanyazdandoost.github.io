People

Research Group

We develop theory and algorithms for large-scale optimization — saddle point and minimax problems, bilevel optimization, and distributed methods — with applications in machine learning and engineering systems.

Current members

Mohammad Mahdi Ahmadi

Ph.D. Student · Systems & Industrial Engineering

Works on saddle point problems with bilevel structure and simultaneous learning and optimization via misspecified saddle point problems. Lead author of papers in the Journal of Scientific Computing.

Cody Melcher

Ph.D. Student · Systems & Industrial Engineering

Works on minimax (saddle point) optimization, including semi-infinite nonconvex constrained min-max problems — lead author of a NeurIPS 2025 paper — and co-instructor of the TinyML outreach workshop.

L. Heitt

Undergraduate Researcher

Undergraduate research on constrained min-max optimization; co-author of the NeurIPS 2025 paper Semi-infinite Nonconvex Constrained Min-Max Optimization.

Alumni

Nazanin Abolfazli, Ph.D.

Ph.D. Alumna → Operations Research Consultant, American Airlines

Doctoral research on bilevel optimization and accelerated distributed methods, with publications in NeurIPS, AISTATS, TMLR, and CISS.

Prospective students

I am always looking for motivated Ph.D. students with a strong background in optimization, applied mathematics, or related areas. If you are interested in large-scale optimization and its applications in machine learning, email me a brief statement of interest, your CV, and transcripts.
